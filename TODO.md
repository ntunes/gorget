- **C codegen: nested-struct drop emits `memcmp` against the parent struct's size, not the field's** — surfaced 2026-04-27 building gorget-arena. Fix: use `sizeof(field_type)` not `sizeof(parent_type)` when emitting the drop guard. [added: 2026-04-27]
