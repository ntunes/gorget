- **MED — a heading inventory does not pin clauses. That is the design.** `AGENTS.md` is held by a size ceiling and a heading-id inventory (`tests/lints.rs::agents_md_size_ratchet`, `agents_md_heading_inventory_is_pinned`). Rewording a heading line after its required prefix is free; deleting a heading or adding an unlisted one is RED. Clause-level probes (the 469-row inventory, sentence census, unpinned-run ratchet, insertion sweep, `scripts/agents_md_crossval.py`) are retired. Bold-lead-in clauses (`**Cardinal rule:**`, gauntlet lead-ins) are not extractor hits. Do not gold-plate `####` / `0bb.` / `1)` or grow a new clause-probe suite to close this item.

  Verify: `cargo test --test lints agents_md_heading_inventory_is_pinned agents_md_size_ratchet`.
