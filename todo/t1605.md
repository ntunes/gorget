  R51's Tracks I and K were **absorbed and then stood down**. Their briefs named two residuals that no surviving item covers:

  ## (a) `Item::Trait` SIGNATURES ARE NOT WALKED BY THE VALIDATION PASS

  The borrow-participant computation is **a selection, not a total enumeration** — it does not reach trait method signatures. ⇒ a `Ref`-containing type spelled there has **no participant flag → no origin → no reject**.

  ⚠ **DISCRIMINATED FROM ITS TWO NEAREST NEIGHBOURS, so this is not a third filing of one fact:**
  - **`[[t1558]]`**'s successor design widens the same computation to **enum variants, tuple elements and post-substitution instantiations** — it does **not** mention `Item::Trait`. Widening as written still misses this.
  - **`[[t1081]]`** is `Item::Trait` in **ggdef's `elaborate`** — a *different pass in a different crate*. Same two words, unrelated subject.

  ## (b) TWO NEGATIVE CONTROLS ARE GREEN FOR UNTRACED REASONS

  `Option[Ref[T]]` and `Callable[…(Ref[T])]` both reach the **correct** answer at HEAD — and **nobody traced why**. Two separate scouts used them as **negative controls** (the shapes a guard must *not* fire on).

  ⛔ **A control whose green is unexplained is SIX-Q #6 waiting to happen:** if either is correct for a reason unrelated to the property under test, it will keep passing after the property breaks. **Trace both before relying on them again** — the two paths are believed distinct and neither has been walked.

  **INTENDED:** the participant walk covers every item kind that can carry a `Ref`-containing type in a signature; and each negative control's correctness is explained by a traced mechanism rather than an observed value.
