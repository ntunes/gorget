- **[MED — Core #2 name-matching inside the census tool, and it makes `--fast` a lie] `scripts/known_gaps_census.sh`'s `is_sh_row` decides "does this row drive the self-host lowerer?" by GREPPING THE TEST BODY FOR THREE HELPER NAMES** — `assert_self_host_stdout`, `self_host_emit_cc_run`, `build_gg_dir_cached`. Any roster row that reaches the driver through a helper NOT on that list is invisible to it, so `--fast` runs it anyway and still pays the ~90 s `driver.gg` build. That is the layering chapter's rule 2 violated at a tool boundary — a semantic fact ("this row is expensive") inferred from an identifier string — which is the same defect class R47 F1/F1-ii retired in the runtime-parity harness one directory over (`runtime_parity_excluded` is gone; the fixture now declares `#!parity-excluded <kind>: <evidence>` and `fixture_parity_exclusion` reads it without ever seeing the stem).
  - **REPRODUCE** (no count is quoted here on purpose — the roster changes whenever anyone files a gap, which is exactly why the script's cost figures had to be replaced with commands): for each row of `scripts/known_gaps_census.sh --list`, take its `fn` body out of `tests/integration.rs`, ask whether it transitively reaches `build_gg_dir_cached`, and compare against what `is_sh_row` matches. The delta is the blind set.
  - **SIZE — 13 blind rows, MEASURED with a name-free instrument, not counted by hand.** Method, because the method is what makes the number trustworthy: parse every top-level `fn` body out of `tests/integration.rs`, take the set of fns whose body mentions `build_gg_dir_cached`, close it transitively over call sites until it stops growing, then ask of each roster row whether it reaches that set — and compare against what `is_sh_row`'s three-name grep matches. The delta is the blind set. ⚠ **Re-derive it rather than trusting this figure**: the roster moves whenever anyone files a gap, and this item's own siblings (`t0824`, and five copies of this script's cost figure) are what a transcribed count does over time.
  - ⚠ **THE COUNT'S HISTORY IS PART OF THE FINDING.** Three passes over the same defect produced **4**, **10** and **13**, and the first two were both wrong for the SAME reason the defect is a defect: they enumerated HELPER NAMES by hand. Only the transitive closure — which asks no questions about names — settled it. A name-based instrument cannot measure the blind spot of a name-based instrument. ⊕ `assert_self_host_rejects_comprehension` is NOT among the blind rows despite reaching the driver: it contributes zero roster rows, and including it was one of the hand-enumeration errors.
  - **Consequence, and it is why this is worth fixing rather than noting:** the script's own header advertised `--fast` at 14 s, a figure that was never reachable while the blind set was non-empty, and that figure survived in FIVE places.
