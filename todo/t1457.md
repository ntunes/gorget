  Concrete: `fs_ops` line 10 flips `true → false` (the trailing `rmdir`); other trials flip line 1 (`mkdir`).

  ⛔ **THE RACERS ALREADY EXIST.** `fn readdir()` and `fn fs_ops()` are plain `#[test]` with **no `#[serial]`**, and `self_host_runtime` executes the emitted binary with no `current_dir` — same `/tmp` paths, same CWD. **This is live today, independent of any new test.**

  ⚠ **WHY IT IS RANKED HIGH RATHER THAN AS FLAKE HYGIENE:** the failure is a **stdout mismatch against a committed snapshot**, so it presents as *"the compiler's output changed"* — **infra noise wearing a correctness verdict's label.** It is indistinguishable from a genuine staleness or miscompile, and it invites retry-and-suppress on exactly the signal that must never be suppressed.

  **Two independent remedies, neither sufficient alone:** `.current_dir(<per-fixture temp dir>)` fixes `readdir`'s crate-root writes but **not** the 8 absolute `/tmp` paths; `#[serial]` on a driver group excludes `self_host_runtime` but **not** the plain-`#[test]` siblings. ⇒ the durable fix is either per-execution path namespacing in the fixtures themselves, or a shared-resource serial group that names all of them.
