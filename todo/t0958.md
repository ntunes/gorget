- **🆕🧹 [MED — SIX QUESTIONS #2, a guard that cannot catch its own class ON THE LANE THE CLASS KEEPS RECURRING ON; SPLIT out of R48 Track U by its confirming brief-review (M5), filed 2026-09-03] The Core #10 silent-fallthrough ratchet is Rust-only, and the self-host lowerer — where the class just cost a real miscompile — is invisible to it.**
  `tests/lints.rs::ratchet_a_lowering_dispatch_silent_fallthrough` walks a `FILES` list of three `src/ir/lowering/stmts/*.rs` paths, and its detector `silent_catchall_arm_lines` matches exactly `_ => {}`, `_ => ()`, `_ => (),`, `_ => {},` and the multi-line `_ => {` … `}` block. A Gorget `else:` followed by `pass` matches none of those spellings and lives in none of those files. **That is why R48 Track U's defect survived**: the self-host `ECall` callee dispatcher matched four callee shapes and dropped every other one — the whole call, arguments included — through an `else: pass`, and no guard in the tree could see it.
  **⚠ THE NAIVE PORT IS WORSE THAN NOTHING, and this is the reason the work was split out rather than bolted on at round close.** **MEASURED 2026-09-03** (`else:` immediately followed by `pass`, exact line count):
  | scope | `else:` / `pass` pairs |
  |---|---|
  | `tests/fixtures/self_host_lowerer/lower_expr.gg` | **81** |
  | `tests/fixtures/self_host_lowerer/*.gg` | **631** |
  A ratchet seeded over that population needs a ~600-entry allowlist, and **if the `ECall` callee arm lands IN that allowlist the guard green-lights the exact class it was written to retire** — the failure mode SIX QUESTIONS #2 names. Most of the 631 are not the user-syntax-drop class at all (detection walkers, `match` on an ownership tag with one interesting case, optional-field probes), so an unscoped count is dominated by noise and would ratchet on the wrong thing.
  **TWO CANDIDATE SHAPES, both from the reviewer that split this out. Neither is chosen; scout them.**
  1. **Scope to DISPATCH arms, not to `else:`.** Detect the shape that matters — a `match` on a CALLEE or a lowering TARGET whose fall-through returns a fresh unit/placeholder local rather than lowering or rejecting. Narrow enough that the allowlist is small enough to read, which is what makes a burn-down honest. Needs a way to identify "this match dispatches user syntax", and the cheap one is a marker comment at the arm — which `todo/t0573` independently argues for on the Rust side (that item's complaint is that the existing ratchet keys its subject on a FUNCTION NAME, so a rename silently relocates the guard's subject). **The two items want the same mechanism; solve them together.**
  2. **A per-file COUNT ratchet, seeded POST-fix.** Freeze today's count per self-host file and let it only shrink. Cheap and spelling-robust, but it says nothing about WHICH arm, so a fixed arm and a fresh one cancel out. Only acceptable with the `ECall` callee arm demonstrably ABSENT from the seed — i.e. seeded after R48 Track U — and even then it is the weaker of the two.
  **VERIFY THE VERIFIER (Core #13).** Whatever lands must be shown RED against the pre-Track-U `lower_expr.gg`, whose `ECall` arm carries the exact `else:` / `pass` this guard exists to catch: `git show <pre-U commit>:tests/fixtures/self_host_lowerer/lower_expr.gg`. A guard that is green on that file has not been demonstrated to work.
  **RELATED, NOT DUPLICATE.** `todo/t0043` asks the RUST ratchet to also see a bare `return` fall-through (a third spelling of the same class, on the lane the ratchet already covers). `todo/t0573` asks it to stop keying its subject on a function name. This item is about the LANE: the self-host `.gg` sources are outside the guard's world entirely.
  **INTENDED:** a guard that goes RED on the pre-Track-U `ECall` callee arm and stays green on the self-host's legitimate `else: pass` arms, with a burn-down set small enough to read.
