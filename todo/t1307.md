### ⚠ ADDENDUM 2026-09-05 — THE PREMISE OF THIS ITEM IS FALSE (R50 CoW design scout, measured)

**This item was filed saying the stored-borrow path "is working *very* well — it is FAST". It is
not, and never was.** The measurement, not a source-read:

- `DictIter[K,V].next()` deep-clones the **entire Dict** on *every* extern accessor call — 4 clones
  per element; `SetIter` / keys / values 3 — and frees none of them.
- `Dict[int,int]` at N=200 000 is **OOM-killed (rc 137)**. At N=2 000 it is 99 ms / 385 MB against
  the builtin `for`'s 0.7 ms / 1.3 MB.
- Clone counts are **identical at the introducing commit (2026-04-25) and every commit since**.
  **The pointer walk never existed.** The `DONE.md` entry claiming "no allocation beyond the
  iterator struct" was a source-read, not a run.

**This is already filed as [[t0952]] (MED), and admitted on ten `LEAK_ALLOWLIST.txt` rows** — the
sanitizer is green *by admission*, which is why nothing surfaced it. Regenerate:

```bash
grep -nE '^(borrow_field_lazy_dict_iter|dict_keys_lazy|dict_values_lazy|set_filter_count|set_take_values|stdlib_iter_dict|stdlib_iter_set)' \
  tests/sanitize/LEAK_ALLOWLIST.txt
```

**Consequence for this item's own scope.** The owner's constraint — *"whatever we replace it with
must be equally fast"* — is satisfied by a very wide margin, because **the bar it names is not the
one that exists.** The scout's 35-line prototype at the *write site* (the generic collector
degrading a declared `Ref[T]` param to `Unit`/`ByValue`, one layer above where `t0952` points)
measures **zero clones and zero leak**, making the `Ref`-field spelling 1.3–3× the builtin loop with
no allocations. The D41-literal owned spelling costs exactly **one map clone per `.iter()`** — which
is precisely what `D52`'s unbuilt bind-elision removes.

**So D41 does not need to bend.** Keep this item LOW/documentation as the owner graded it; the
*defect* is `t0952`, whose severity is the thing that was mis-set.

  ## ⭐ THE RECONCILIATION, FOUND IN GIT HISTORY (owner asked 2026-09-06; recovered from `c9a3851ec`, 2026-04-25)

  **The stored `Ref` field was not an oversight — it was introduced deliberately, with a stated safety property.** From the commit that created it (`feat(stdlib): switch Dict.iter()/Set.iter() to lazy bucket-walk`):

  > *"The yielded keys/values come out of `gorget_map_iter_key`/`_value` which **clone resource-typed K/V via the map's `key_clone`/`val_clone` hooks, so callers may freely drop the pair without disturbing the source map.**"*

  and, on why the field must **alias** rather than clone:

  > *"For `Ref[T]`/`MutRef[T]` borrow fields (which ARE `Ptr` at the IR level), **the field semantics is 'alias the source'** … Without this, `Dict.iter()` would clone the dict inside the `iter()` body, store the address of a stack-local clone in `DictIter.source`, and **return a dangling pointer**."*

  ⇒ ⭐ **THE SAFETY ARGUMENT HAS TWO CONDITIONS, AND ONLY THE FIRST IS DISCHARGED:**
  1. ✅ **Nothing borrowed ESCAPES.** Every value `next()` yields is an **owned clone**, so no borrow reaches the caller. Established by the commit, still true.
  2. ❓ **The view must not OUTLIVE its source.** Partially guarded: `E_DanglingReturn` catches the **return** case via `struct_field_ref_flags` / `origins.rs:204`; **escape-by-assignment and source-moved-away are NOT caught** (measured, R51 Track K's scout).

  ## WHY THIS RECONCILES D41 RATHER THAN BENDING IT
  `docs/internals/cow-transient-view-model.md:183` states D41's rationale exactly: *"**Transience is enforced by the type being unspellable in any resting position, NOT BY ANALYSIS.**"* ⇒ **a stdlib FIELD is a resting position, so unspellability cannot be the enforcement there** — which is precisely why the stdlib needs conditions (1) and (2) instead.
  ⇒ **The coherent split: unspellability remains the USER-facing rule (no analysis needed, exactly as D41 argues); the builtin-internal fields are permitted and their safety is discharged by (1) + (2), with (2) supplied by ANALYSIS.**

  ## ⛔ AND LAZINESS IS A PUBLISHED PROMISE, NOT A PERF NUMBER
  `docs/book/05-collections.md:270`: *"`Set.iter()` and `Dict.iter()` are **lazy** bucket walks — no materialisation of `.items()` first"*, with `for x in s.iter().take(2)` documented as *"walks two buckets, stops."* ⇒ **migrating the four fields to owned would break a documented user-facing guarantee**, on top of the measured **O(n) per adapter stage** (a 4-stage chain = 4 full collection clones per loop).

  ⊕ **ONE PIECE OF WORK DISCHARGES BOTH THIS AND `[[t1558]]`:** condition (2) needs the **same** origin/participant machinery `t1558`'s successor design names — extend it past the return case, and past `Item::Struct`-only, to escape-by-assignment, source-moved-away, and the enum/tuple/substitution carriers.
