- **🆕🧹 [MED — a SPLIT DECISION the reference compiler does not have, and R48 Track U's blocking defect was downstream of it; filed 2026-09-03 by that track] The self-host decides "is this an implicit-`it` body?" TWICE, in two places, with two different traversals.**
  * `tests/fixtures/self_host_typechecker/parser.gg::expr_contains_it` — checks only the TOP LEVEL (`EIt` / `EIdentifier("it")`) and decides whether to WRAP the argument in `EImplicitClosure`.
  * `tests/fixtures/self_host_lowerer/lower_closures.gg::expr_has_it` — a deep traversal, and it decides whether to TREAT an unwrapped argument as a body.
  Rust `gg` makes this decision ONCE, at the parser. Here the parser's shallow answer means most implicit-`it` bodies arrive at the lowerer UNWRAPPED, so the lowerer's predicate is load-bearing for all of them — and when the two disagree the argument is treated as a CALLABLE and wrapped `(body)(__hofp0)`, which is a miscompile, not a missed optimisation. **R48 Track U's blocking defect was exactly this**: `expr_has_it` named 8 of `Expr`'s 44 variants, so `xs.map(it.n)` SEGV'd and `xs.filter(it.n > 1)` produced a silently EMPTY vector. That round made `expr_has_it` exhaustive, which fixes the instances; it does not close the SPLIT, and the next variant added to `Expr` re-opens it in exactly the same way.
  ⚠ **THE SHALLOW SIDE IS DEFENDED BY AN UNCITED COMMENT, WHICH SELF-HOST RULE 1 SAYS TO VERIFY OR DELETE.** Verbatim at `expr_contains_it`: *"Avoiding deep traversal of borrowed Expr to prevent double-free from pattern-extracted fields (Box/Vector) being dropped at scope exit."* No item is cited, no fixture pins it, and the sibling `expr_has_it` in the lowerer **does** traverse the same shapes — its own header says *"this traverses owned values — safe to recurse"* — and does not double-free. So either the bug is real and the lowerer's deep walk is unsound for a reason nobody has written down, or it was fixed and the workaround is a fossil. **Both readings are actionable and the current state is neither.**
  **THE REFERENCE-GRADE SHAPE** is the reference's: decide ONCE. Either the parser wraps every implicit-`it` body in `EImplicitClosure` (deleting the lowerer's predicate as a consumer), or the parser stops trying and the lowerer's walk is the single documented decision. Do not keep two.
  **FIRST STEP, and it is cheap:** verify the double-free claim. Make `expr_contains_it` deep, run the self-host lane and the bootstrap under ASan, and see whether anything double-frees. If nothing does, delete the comment and the shallowness with it — that alone collapses the split. If something does, the comment gets a cited item and a fixture, and the split becomes a documented trade-off instead of an unexplained one.
  **DISCRIMINATED from `todo/t0958`** — that item is about a GUARD being blind to a lane (the silent-fallthrough ratchet cannot see `.gg`). This one is about the COMPILER having two answers to one question. `t0958`'s guard would not have caught this; `expr_has_it` returned a wrong `false`, not a silent fall-through.
