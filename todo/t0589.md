- **🆕🐛 [macOS dev-box false RED] `security_safe_no_leak` mis-reports on any platform without LeakSanitizer.** The 5 `cow_bareassign_*_no_leak` tests fail on macOS with `AddressSanitizer: detect_leaks is not supported on this platform` — the harness's `has_leak_report` scrape (`tests/security.rs:271-275`) matches the substring `"AddressSanitizer"`, which that very *unsupported* notice contains, so the guard fires on its own inapplicability. Real leaks are still gated on Linux/CI. Fix: detect the unsupported notice and SKIP (loudly) rather than fail, or gate the no-leak assertions on `cfg!(target_os = "linux")` the way `spec_conformance`'s `floor_active` already does — a permanently-red local suite trains contributors to ignore it.
