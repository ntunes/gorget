- **[MED — the reference-grade instrument for the borrow-into-an-owning-destination class; filed 2026-09-04 by R49 Track N1, which shipped a text ratchet that is NOT sufficient] A BIR-validator borrow-provenance check.**
  - **WHAT EXISTS TODAY:** `tests/lints.rs::hof_borrowed_elem_ptr_sinks_are_cloning` — a source-text ratchet over `src/bir/lower.rs` pinning, as a keyed multiset, the SINK of every read of a borrowed collection-element pointer, plus the `gorget_array_adopt_hooks` call-site count and the context-struct brace-form count. It is bidirectional, fails CLOSED (a read whose sink it cannot identify is a violation, not an exemption), and was measured RED on **eleven compiling mutants**, including a new sibling expander that binds the scaffold under a name other than `ctx` and one that DESTRUCTURES it.
  - ⛔ **IT IS NOT SUFFICIENT, AND THE FIRST VERSION OF THIS ITEM SAID IT WAS.** That claim was written from a mutant set built in the guard's own image; a fresh reviewer invented two the executor had not shown it and **both were GREEN on compiling code that was exactly the class** — the guard's read pattern was anchored on the receiver name `ctx.`, which every existing expander happens to use. Both holes are now closed and both mutants are RED, but the episode is the reason this item exists and the reason the sufficiency claim does not come back. ⚡ **A guard measured only against probes its own author designed has been measured for consistency, not for coverage.**
  - **WHAT IT STILL CANNOT DO, precisely:** it reads ONE FILE as TEXT, and it sees a borrow only where the source spells a FIELD ACCESS. A value threaded out of a helper's return, carried in a tuple, or dropped into a `Vec<ValueId>` assembled elsewhere reaches a consuming position with no `.elem_ptr` anywhere for it to read. It also reads SINKS, not behaviour: a mutation that keeps the sink spelling and corrupts the arguments passes it (measured — and the fixtures catch that one, 8 of 18 cells red). A validator carrying the provenance has none of those limits.
  - **WHY IT WAS NOT ATTEMPTED IN R49:** it needs a typed borrow tag the BIR does not carry — `Inst::CallExtern` and `Inst::CallClosure` both take a bare `Vec<ValueId>` on one shared enum, so there is nothing at the instruction level distinguishing "pointer into the source" from any other pointer. Adding the tag is the real work, and `src/bir/validate.rs` was another track's zone that round.
  - **WHY A TYPE DOES NOT SUBSTITUTE (measured, do not re-litigate):** a newtype over the pointer field hides it (`E0616`) but the defect walks straight through whatever accessor the BENIGN role needs, and the benign role is 26 of the 33 reads. A single `clone_into_owned()` as the only route to a consuming position DOUBLE-CLONES at four of the seven, because `gorget_map_put_cloned` calls `gorget_map_put` and THEN deep-clones in place (`src/backend/c/runtime/runtime_map.c`). The roles are three, not two.
  - **WHEN THIS LANDS:** the text ratchet becomes redundant and should be removed in the same change, not left as a second source of truth.
