- **🆕🔧 [MED — the ledger axis that can be reduced by HIDING its members. Found 2026-09-08 by the debt-ledger output-review.]**

  `scripts/convergence.sh`'s `lints_guard_constants` enumerator keys on a DECLARED constant:

      grep -cE '^\s*(pub )?const [A-Za-z_0-9]+\s*:[^=]*=\s*[0-9_]+\s*;' tests/lints.rs

  An **inline** guard value has no declaration and is therefore not counted. Two pre-existing
  siblings, both real pins: `tests/lints.rs:11705` (`assert_eq!(ids.len(), 3, …)`) and
  `tests/lints.rs:28222` (`assert_eq!(anchors.len(), 4, …)`). Regenerate the population:
  `grep -nE 'assert_eq!\([^,]+,\s*[0-9]+\s*,' tests/lints.rs`.

  ⚠ **The axis is the one whose honest target is 0** (AGENTS.md Round lifecycle step 5: guard
  constants are purely our own artifact, nobody discovers them). An under-counting enumerator on
  that axis is worse than on any other, because **moving a declared constant inline reads as a
  burn-down** — progress the ledger reports and nobody made.

  **INTENDED:** the axis counts every pinned guard value in `tests/lints.rs`, declared or inline,
  so the row cannot be lowered by relocation. The keying must stay STABLE across unrelated edits
  (today's keys are `<enclosing fn>::<CONST>`; a line number would churn every round).

  ⊕ Cheap and honest interim if the inline census proves noisy: name the omission in the ledger's
