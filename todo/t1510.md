  Measured under the receiver-place fix, `Box[float]` carrying `2.5`:

  | read shape | owned local | struct field | plain param |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `(*b)` | `2.500000` ✅ | `2.500000` ✅ | `2.500000` ✅ |
  | `.get()` | `2` ✗ | `2` ✗ | `2.000000` ✗ |

  ⇒ **THE DEFECT IS IN THE READ SHAPE, NOT THE PAYLOAD TYPE OR THE RECEIVER PLACE.** `[[t1456]]` files it as *"a payload-type axis defect"*; that mechanism is **wrong**, and a fixture built to it would sit green forever (the `t1359` failure mode). ⛔ **Correct `t1456`'s mechanism; do not open a second item for the same defect.**

  ⊕ **A THIRD CELL, IN NO ENUMERATION ANYWHERE:** `Box[bool](false)` through a **struct field** prints **`0`** under the fix — an integer, not a bool. It CHANGES under the fix and stays WRONG.
  ⊕ **AND THE CONSEQUENCE IS GOOD NEWS FOR COVERAGE:** cells previously written off as *"selected but still wrong, therefore unpinnable"* **ARE pinnable — with `(*b)`**, which is also exactly what `[[t0685]]`'s binding LLVM constraint demands for non-primitive payloads. A row is not unpinnable because one read shape is broken; **pin it through the shape that works and name the other.**
