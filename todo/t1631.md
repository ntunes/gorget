- **🆕🔧 [MED — GUARD STRENGTH AVAILABLE FOR FREE: no new mechanism, no `--bless`, no owner decision. Found 2026-09-09 by the guard witness cut.]**

  A pinned magnitude needs a constant somebody maintains; a **witness** asserts two independently-derived readings agree and needs no constant at all, so it cannot rot and its breakage names the broken instrument instead of reading as progress. Four conversions are available **today**, each verified by the cut:

  1. **`formatter_ownership_modifier_site_pin`** — a second in-tree enumerator of the same sites already exists; the pin simply never consults it.
  2. **`self_host_infer_bool_arms_publish_chain_link`** — same shape, same fix.
  3. **`parity_untriaged_exclusions_shrink_only`** — `tests/integration.rs::fixture_parity_exclusion` is a **separately-written parser of the same `#!parity-excluded untriaged:` declaration**, and nothing compares the two parsed sets. **One `comm -13` away from a true witness.**
  4. ⭐ **`interp_error_retention_arms_count` → rustc exhaustiveness** — the only strength-1 (MECHANISM) upgrade in the whole population. Replace the `matches!` whitelist with a catch-all-free `match`, and a new variant becomes a **compile error** rather than a count somebody must remember to bump. An in-tree template for exactly this shape already exists.

  ⚠ **TWO TRAPS A WITNESS BUILT HERE WOULD WALK INTO, both measured:**
  - `scripts/known_gaps_census.sh` emits **phantom stems** from its `known_gaps/[A-Za-z0-9_]+` regex biting off glob/brace mentions in `tests/integration.rs`. They do not inflate the roster (each shares a row with a real stem and `ledger_members` reads column 1) but they **pollute the fixture column** — so a witness built on that column trips on them. Fix the regex first.
  - The self-host closure-identity counter uses a recursive walk that **follows symlinks**, while the self-host name-prefix counter explicitly **skips** them to avoid double-counting shared `.gg` files. Today no hit lands in a symlinked file, so the divergence is latent; a hit in `ast.gg`/`parser.gg`/`typecheck.gg` would be counted once **per self-host directory that links it**. Two counters over the same tree must agree on symlink policy or they are not witnesses for each other.

  ⛔ **AND ANTI-VACUITY FLOORS ARE NOT WITNESSES.** `code_doc_citations_resolve`, `fmt_no_new_over_budget_lines`, `owning_param_ctor_move_helper_site_count` and `no_growth_in_self_host_lower_case_none_pass_stubs` all carry a lower floor that catches an enumerator going **silent** — none catches one that is merely **maimed**. That is `scripts/convergence.sh`'s own documented lesson: narrowing its `ignored_tests` pattern reported near-total "progress" at rc 0. Do not count a floor as the second reading.
