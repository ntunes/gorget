- **[MED — R47 Track F5a] Wire `scripts/clone_meter_check.sh --anchor-age` into the round-open step.
  Today it is a guard whose only caller is a sentence.**

  **WHAT EXISTS.** The four clone ratchets carry TWO numbers per meter: an exact `..._CLONE_PIN`
  that moves at every integration, and a `..._CLONE_ROUND_OPEN` anchor that the ~1% authorization
  band and the >1% owner ask are evaluated against. The anchor is re-seeded **once per round, at
  round open**. `--anchor-age` detects a missed re-seed: a round CLOSES by adding a dated entry at
  the top of `DONE.md`, so a `DONE.md` entry newer than the `// ROUND-OPEN-DATE:` line beside the
  anchors means a round boundary was crossed without re-seeding. It exits non-zero and says what to
  do. RED-verified in both directions (stale anchor, and a missing date line).

  **WHAT IS MISSING.** `grep -rn "clone_meter_check.sh" TODO.md AGENTS.md todo/ docs/` returns
  nothing outside this item: **no procedure runs any mode of that script.** So a forgotten
  re-anchor is still silent, and the failure it would catch is not cosmetic — it silently converts
  the per-round band into the **cross-round accumulation the owner explicitly rejected**
  (2026-08-30: *"keep slow accumulation unbounded… but we should do periodic audits"*, with a
  cumulative cap ruled out). R48 legitimately growing +0.9% on top of R47's +0.5% would read as
  +1.4% against a stale anchor and manufacture a **false owner ask**.

  **THE FIX IS ONE LINE IN A PROCEDURE, and it belongs to whoever owns the round lifecycle.**
  Round lifecycle step 1 (*"Open a round around a headline theme"*) gains: *re-seed the four
  `..._CLONE_ROUND_OPEN` constants and `ROUND-OPEN-DATE` from the round-open measurement, then
  `bash scripts/clone_meter_check.sh --anchor-age` must pass.* ⊕ The re-seed itself costs no new
  build when the previous round's close measurement is to hand — the round-open value IS the
  previous round's closing measurement.

  ⚠ **DELIBERATELY NOT A LINT, and do not "fix" it by making it one.** A lint would go RED in the
  window between a round adding its `DONE.md` entry and the next round re-anchoring — i.e. it would
  red the records commit that closes a round. A permanently-red ratchet stops being a ratchet, which
  is the tree's own rationale on these four constants
  (`tests/integration.rs`, `const SELF_COMPILE_ARRAY_CLONE_PIN`). The hard, always-safe half IS
  already a lint: `tests/lints.rs::clone_meter_pins_carry_their_provenance` asserts the four anchors
  share ONE sha, that it is an ancestor of HEAD, and that exactly one `ROUND-OPEN-DATE` line exists.
  What it cannot see is whether that sha is *this* round's open — "which round is open" is not in
  the tree, which is the whole reason `--anchor-age` reads `DONE.md`.

  ⊕ Same wiring question applies to the script's other two modes, which also have no caller:
  `--track` (the per-track attribution gate an output-review runs, zero-build) and
  `--pin-staleness`. `--track` is at least named in the ratchet's own failure text, so a tripped
  gate points at it; `--anchor-age` is pointed at by nothing that ever runs.
