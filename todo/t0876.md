- **[MED — R48 Track F, declared phase 2 of the figures DB] EACH BURN-DOWN TRACK ADOPTS `scripts/figures.db` FOR ITS OWN ROWS — not a migration track, an absorption.**

  **WHAT ALREADY EXISTS**, so nobody re-derives it: `scripts/figures.db` (the declaration, in
  `scripts/clone_meter.spec`'s restricted dialect with dotted keys), three readers
  (`scripts/figures.sh` awk · `scripts/figures.py` · the `figures_db_*` helpers in `tests/lints.rs`),
  and four enforcing `figures_db_*` lints. `python3 scripts/figures.py --validate` checks the schema contract,
  `--scan` is the uniqueness guard, `--census` prints the duplicate-spelling census, `check <row>`
  runs a row's `regen` and applies its `polarity`, and `report` runs every `informational` row.

  **HOW TO ADOPT A ROW** (MIRROR mode — the literal STAYS in the code):
  1. `row = <subject>.<name>` plus the mandatory fields; `python3 scripts/figures.py --validate`
     names every one you missed.
  2. `mirror = <path>:<SYMBOL>` so `figures_db_mirrors_agree` reds when the two drift.
  3. `python3 scripts/figures.py --scan` and declare every surviving spelling as an EXACT
     `waiver = <path> <count> declared|debt <reason>` — or, better, delete the duplicate and cite
     the constant.

  **⚠ THE THING TO GET RIGHT IS `polarity` × `provenance`, AND THEY ARE ORTHOGONAL.** A row is
  `shrink-only` / `grow-only` / `exact-pin` / `informational` by what a checker DOES with it, and
  `measured` / `derived` / `policy` by where the value CAME FROM. Do not collapse them: `policy`
  crosses more than one polarity, which is precisely why it is not a fifth polarity.

  **⚠ THE POPULATION IS BIGGER AND DIFFERENT FROM THE NAME-SUFFIX SAMPLE.** A `*(CEILING|FLOOR|
  LIMIT|BUDGET|BASELINE)` grep returns a name-shaped SAMPLE: it over-counts vendored SQLite macros
  and misses `SHAPE_MAX_DEPTH`, `FILL_PACK_MAX_WIDTH`, `AGENTS_MD_MAX_UNPINNED_RUN` and every clone
  pin. Regenerate with a declaration-shaped scan instead
  (`grep -rnE '^\s*(pub )?const [A-Z][A-Z0-9_]*: *(u|i)(size|8|16|32|64) *=' tests/ src/ scripts/`)
  and use BOTH: neither instrument can inherit the other's blind spot.

  **⛔ THE LARGEST POPULATION IS THE ONE THE GUARD CANNOT SERVE, AND THAT MUST BE SAID IN THE ROW,
  NOT DISCOVERED.** The `EXPECTED_*` arm pins in `tests/` are the biggest single family and their
  values are single- and double-digit integers. The uniqueness scanner cannot police a value that
  common, so those rows take `scan = none` and their whole enforcement is `figures_db_mirrors_agree`
  plus the mandatory `regen`. Write the `caveat` accordingly — a row whose caveat claims more than
  its `scan` field delivers is worse than no row.

  **⛔ WHAT NEVER BECOMES A ROW.** `docs/define-gorget/decisions.md` (owner-only, and a dated
  measurement inside a ratified entry is evidence for the ruling AT THE TIME, not a live figure) ·
  `DONE.md` (a round record is history) · the 22 vendored SQLite macros · `SHAPE_MAX_DEPTH`
  (terminal — nothing reproduces it, and a fake `regen` would poison the mandatory field for every
  other row) · timeouts and `Cargo.toml` versions. A census figure with NO standing command is also
  refused, by construction: `regen` is mandatory. That is the schema working, not a gap.

  trigger wired into `AGENTS.md`), [[t0850]], [[t0574]].
