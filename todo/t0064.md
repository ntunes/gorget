- **🆕🐛 [MED-HIGH — `gg check` PASSES then C EMIT REFERENCES AN UNDECLARED FUNCTION; measured 2026-07-26] Binding `.enumerate()` to a local emits a call to a nonexistent runtime symbol.** `Vector[int] v = [1,2,3]; auto e = v.enumerate()` → `gg check` clean, then the C backend emits `gorget_array_enumerate(...)` and the compile dies: *"implicit declaration of function 'gorget_array_enumerate'; did you mean 'gorget_array_insert'?"*. So `.enumerate()` works **inline in a for-loop** but not when bound. ⚠ **Design-relevant, not just a gap:** the pre-binding form (`auto e = a.enumerate(); for b in &e:`) is the natural workaround for the for-loop adapter ambiguity — and it is doubly dead, because even if it compiled, `D41` (no stored borrows; elaborated in `docs/internals/cow-transient-view-model.md` § "Articulation with #13") ratifies that **BINDS MATERIALIZE**, so `&e` would write through to a private copy and never reach `a`. Own small track; likely a missing runtime shim or a lowering that should reject/inline the bind.
