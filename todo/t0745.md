- **🆕 [LOW — ggdef subset gap, Core #9's "out-of-subset shapes get a note + a filed subset gap"; filed by R47 Track D1 as the fourth-lane half of its cross-lane pin] ggdef cannot adjudicate the primitive-receiver `E_NoMethodFound` reject.** A method that exists on no builtin table reaches `elaborate`'s builtin arm-picker catch-all (`spec/ggdef/src/elaborate/mod.rs:2745` — ``method `.{other}()` is outside the phase-0 subset (may need Increment B2)``). That is an `ElabError`, i.e. a `FrontendError`, which the ggdef run-surface lane records as **GGDEF-SKIP, not MATCH** (`spec/ggdef/tests/spec_conformance_ggdef.rs`), so `ggdef gen` cannot own the expect block.
  - Concretely: `"to_string"` appears NOWHERE in `spec/ggdef/src/` (`grep -rn '"to_string"' spec/ggdef/src/` → no hits), so ggdef refuses `3.0.to_string()` for the RIGHT REASON BY ACCIDENT — "outside my subset" rather than "no such method". A shape that is out of subset and a shape that is genuinely absent are different verdicts, and today the lane cannot tell them apart.
  - The two fixtures this blocks carry `adjudicator: production-v1` and say so inline: `spectests/run/reject_no_method_on_float.gg`, `spectests/run/reject_no_method_on_string.gg`. C, LLVM and self-host all adjudicate them; ggdef abstains.
  - Closing it means giving `elaborate` a real builtin-method table for primitive receivers so an ABSENT method rejects as `E_NoMethodFound` rather than as a subset error — at which point both fixtures can move to the generated adjudicator and `GGDEF_MATCH_FLOOR` rises by two.
