- **🆕🛡 [MED — THE PRESCRIBED PROTECTION AGAINST A LOAD ARTEFACT DOES NOT EXIST, AND THE ARTEFACT IT WOULD PREVENT IS SELF-ERASING. Found by R50 Track K's executor while seeding 93 cell-lanes on a box at load 5.7–10.7 / 10 cores.]**

  **(a) THE KNOBS ARE NOT READ.** Regenerate: `grep -n "getenv\|environ" scripts/robustness_map.py` → **one hit, `ASAN_OPTIONS`**, and `grep -n "GG_BUILD_TIMEOUT_SECS\|GG_TEST_TIMEOUT_SECS" scripts/robustness_map.py` → **nothing**. Every timeout is a literal in the lane runner: `timeout=300` for each build (C, self-host cc, sanitize), `timeout=30` for a value-lane run, `timeout=120` for the ASan run and for ggdef. ⚠ **Briefs and round-close guidance tell the operator to set `GG_BUILD_TIMEOUT_SECS=600 GG_TEST_TIMEOUT_SECS=600` before a map run.** Those exports are read by `scripts/run_integration.sh` and the integration harness; **this script ignores them**, so an operator who sets them believes they bought headroom they did not buy. Either read the knobs or say plainly in the module docstring that the timeouts are fixed — but do not leave a documented mitigation that is a no-op.

  **(b) AND THE ARTEFACT ERASES ITS OWN EVIDENCE — this is the half that matters.** A lane that times out under load buckets `TIMEOUT`. Written into an **empty** column by `--seed-new`, that becomes the baseline. `TIMEOUT` is non-good, so on the next run at normal load the cell measures `WORKS` and the scorer takes `base != good and bucket == good` → **PROGRESS**, folded silently by any routine `--accept`. **The false record is never a REGRESSION, never a DRIFT, and disappears labelled as an improvement.** The mirror case (a good bucket seeded for a flaky cell) is the loud one — it becomes a permanent REGRESSION that neither `--accept` nor `--accept-drift` can fold, because both are gated behind `not regressions`. ⇒ **the DANGEROUS direction is the quiet one, and it is the likely one under load.**

  ⭐ **NOT HYPOTHETICAL AS A CLASS, though it did not fire on the run that found it:** K's four seeding runs produced **zero TIMEOUT and zero UNKNOWN** across all 93 cell-lanes (regenerate the census with `awk -F'\t' 'NR>1 && $3!="CONTROL" {print $8"\n"$10"\n"$11}' tests/fixtures/robustness_map/MANIFEST.tsv | sort | uniq -c`). The reviewing step that would have caught one was a **hand diff of the MANIFEST**, not a guard.

Widening `HANG_CENSUS` does not reach it, because the cell is not expected to hang on any lane.
