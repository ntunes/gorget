- **🆕🐛 [MED — Core #9 ACCEPT/REJECT LANE DIVERGENCE, created by R49 Track C and recorded rather than
  left implicit; measured 2026-09-03 with the self-host lowerer driver] The self-host has no
  method-existence check for a USER-STRUCT receiver, so it accepts what Rust gg now rejects.**
  `Plain p = Plain(1); p.some_bogus_method()` — self-host `gg check` clean, then
  `undefined reference to 'Plain__some_bogus_method'` at link. That is exactly the pre-fix Rust
  behaviour; Rust gg rejects it with a spanned `E_NoMethodFound` since `t1019` landed
  (`tests/fixtures/no_method_on_bare_struct_error.gg`).
  - **WHERE THE HOLE IS.** `reject_no_method_on_primitive`
    (`tests/fixtures/self_host_typechecker/typecheck.gg`) is scoped to PRIMITIVE receivers, driven from
    `RTPrimitive` via `prim_recv_name`; `reject_wrong_receiver_combinator` returns for any receiver
    that is not RTDefined/RTGeneric and carries only its 9 ratified one-sided arms. Neither reaches a
    user struct.
  - **THE DIRECTION IS NOT IN DOUBT — Rust's reject is correct — so this is a lane LAG, not an owner
    ask.** ⊕ **THE PORT'S SHAPE IS PRECEDENTED AND THE SOURCE SAYS SO: a NEW FUNCTION, not a tenth
    arm.** `tests/lints.rs::sh_reject_wrong_receiver_combinator_arms_count` pins that set at 9 and
    stays at 9; `reject_no_method_on_primitive`'s own header explains why an ABSENT method is a
    different class from a method on the wrong side of a two-sided pair.
  - ⚠ **THE RISK TO MEASURE BEFORE LANDING IT, because it is what killed two adjacent attempts on the
    RUST side (57 healthy fixtures rejected) and one on the SH side (12 valid programs refused with a
    bare `RTPrimitive` test):** the rule must NOT reject a method reached through a **trait bound** on
    a generic parameter, a **trait impl**, a **trait default**, `Box[Trait]`, or an **auto-derivable**.
    The Rust rule's discriminators are `DefKind::{Struct,Enum,Newtype}` (excluding `GenericParam`),
    `has_any_impl_by_name`, and a `struct_fields` shadow test; the self-host equivalents are the
    corroboration machinery `prim_recv_corroborated` and `registry_declares_method_anywhere` already
    provide. **Sweep the self-host lane over the corpus and require ZERO new rejections.**
