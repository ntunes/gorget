  **THE MECHANISM.** `self_host_emit_cc_run` runs three subprocesses. Regenerate the two that matter:
  `grep -n 'let emit = run_with_timeout(' tests/integration.rs` (take the hit inside `fn self_host_emit_cc_run`) and `grep -n 'run_with_timeout_catching(&mut run_cmd' tests/integration.rs`. The RUN step is CAUGHT — a hung fixture binary becomes a per-fixture `Crashed` outcome, with a comment explaining exactly why that matters. **The EMIT step is not.** A self-host driver that hangs on one fixture panics, the panic is re-raised by `parallel_map_fixtures`' `.expect("worker panicked")`, and the whole worker's results go with it.

  ⚠ **THE CONSEQUENCE IS THE ONE THE RUN-STEP COMMENT ALREADY ARGUES AGAINST, so this is not a new argument — it is the same argument, unapplied one call up.** Callers inside a `parallel_map_fixtures` closure: `self_host_runtime` (default-running, build-breaking, 1376 snapshots), `self_host_comprehension_net`, `self_host_runtime_diff`, and the regen. A driver hang there yields `worker panicked: Any { .. }` — **no fixture named, and every genuine regression in the same chunk invisible.** Measured for the sibling class: with the plain runner, a hanging fixture planted alongside a real staleness produced ZERO mention of the staleness.
