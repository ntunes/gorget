- **🆕 [MED — FIXED CELLS WITH NO NET, NAMED RATHER THAN QUIETLY DROPPED. Measured 2026-09-06 by R51 Track A's executor.]**

  R51 Track A's pinned set was defined mechanically as *the cells whose stdout differs between the pre-fix and shipping compilers*. **Four of them ship unpinned**, and the reason is a collision between two real constraints, not an oversight:

  | cell | pre-fix (C) | shipping (C) | LLVM, BOTH states |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `Box[String].get()` through a struct field | garbage | `hi` ✅ | **build fails** (`llc`) |
  | `Box[String].get()` through a plain param | **rc 139 SEGV** | `hi` ✅ | **build fails** |
  | `Box[String].get()` through a closure capture | **rc 139 SEGV** | `hi` ✅ | **build fails** |
  | `Box[bool](false).get()` through a plain param | `true` | `false` ✅ | **rc 139 SEGV** |

  ⛔ **A SEGV CLASS WAS CLOSED ON THE C LANE AND NOTHING HOLDS IT.** These are the loudest cells the fix moved, and they are exactly the ones no committed fixture can carry: `run_gg` runs every fixture on whichever backend `GG_BACKEND` selects, so a fixture pinning them **reds the LLVM sweep**. There is no C-lane-only runner in `tests/integration.rs`.

  ⚠ **THE UNDERLYING LLVM DEFECTS ARE FILED SEPARATELY AND ARE NOT THIS ITEM**: `[[t0685]]` (non-primitive `Box` payloads on LLVM, whose binding constraint is *"a regression net that reads the box back with `.get()` cannot be ported to the LLVM lane at all"*) and `[[t1510]]` (the `.get()` read shape). **This item is the COVERAGE consequence**, which neither of them records — closing them is what makes the net writable.

  ⊕ The place cells that DO ship (`tests/fixtures/self_host_gaps/box_receiver_*_get.gg`) use `Box[int]`, which is the one payload that is route-exercising on C *and* portable on LLVM. That was a deliberate choice and it leaves this hole.

  **INTENDED:** either the LLVM lane compiles `Box[String].get()` and stops SEGVing on `Box[bool].get()`, or the harness grows a declared C-lane-only runner so a cell that is genuinely one-lane can still be pinned.
