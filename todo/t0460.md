- **🧹 [LOW — Track I v2, filed 2026-07-28] Widen `W_RecursiveBareParamMaterialize` to MUTUAL recursion.** v1 checks DIRECT self-recursion only (`resolve_callee_def_id(callee) == current_fn_def_id`). A mutual-recursion cycle (`f` calls `g`, `g` calls `f`) that carries a bare param through the cycle exhibits the same materialize-per-frame pathology but v1 misses it. Requires a call-graph pass in the safety checker (SCC on the fn graph, filter to SCCs of size > 1, then classify each cycle-member's params). Sequenced after the direct-self case has soaked in the corpus and the false-positive rate is measured. Files as a widening, not a v1 miss.
