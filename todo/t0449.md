- **🔵 TAIL-CALL OPTIMIZATION (TCO) — LATER (owner-set; a real language feature).** gg has no TCO. **Phase 1 (tractable, high-value): self-tail-recursion → loop** — detect a tail-position call to the SAME function (`return f(args)` returned DIRECTLY; ⚠ `return deep(n-1)+1` is NOT tail) THROUGH if/match/block tails, lower to param-rebind + jump-to-entry. Both compilers (Rust GIR/LIR + self-host `lower.gg`). **Phase 2 (defer): general/sibling tail calls** (`musttail`/trampoline). **Design Qs FIRST:** (a) GUARANTEED (Scheme-like, stack-safe — recommended given the safety pillar) vs best-effort; (b) ⚠ DROP/CoW ordering hazard — the tail jump REUSES the frame, so dead droppable locals must drop BEFORE the jump + a live droppable that outlives the call BLOCKS the TCO (UAF/leak otherwise); (c) the `assert return` postcondition runs AT the return. Non-tail deep recursion (`stack_guard_deep_recursion`) intentionally NOT covered (owner chose honest OS-default-stack). Gate: a tail-recursive fixture runs at 1e7+ depth on an 8MB stack; `fixed_point` GREEN; no ASan drop/leak regression.
