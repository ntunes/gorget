- **What the evidence now says.** The prescan's job is to mark names *mutated later* so a view is materialized at its bind site. The rescue owed here is at the **capture boundary** (a closure capture is an ownership boundary per `AGENTS.md` §Ownership at Consuming Positions), not in the mutation prescan.
