- **🆕🛡 [MED — THE ONLY DETECTOR FOR A WHOLE CLASS IS A 100-MINUTE SWEEP TEST. Measured 2026-09-05 by the R50 orchestrator; it red the round-close battery.]**

  R50 Track A2 repurposed `tests/fixtures/newtype.gg` — a fixture that CONSTRUCTED a newtype and then printed a **constant string**, `print("newtype works")`, never reading the payload, so it was structurally incapable of catching the very defect it was named for (SIX-Q #6). A2 correctly changed it to `print(id.0)` and updated the live Rust-lane assertion to `run_gg("newtype.gg", "42")`. **It did not re-seed `tests/fixtures/runtime_snapshots/newtype.out`**, which still read `newtype works`.

  Consequence: `self_host_runtime` — the 1376-snapshot lock-in net — reported `newtype: WRONG-OUTPUT (L0: oracle="newtype works" self="42")` and took the C sweep to **rc 101 after 6070s**. The self-host was RIGHT; the committed snapshot was stale.

  ⚠ **THE DEFECT IS THE DETECTION LATENCY, NOT A2's MISS.** Four separate gates could not see it: the executor runs targeted tests (MA-4 puts sweeps on the parent), the output-review reads a diff in which the two files simply look unrelated, integration runs `--lib`/`lints`/census/staging, and the coupling is implicit — a fixture at `tests/fixtures/` top level AUTO-JOINS the snapshot corpus with nothing in either file naming the other.

  **Regenerate the absence of a guard:** `grep -n runtime_snapshots tests/lints.rs` → three DOC-COMMENT mentions, zero assertions.

  **Shape of the guard (Core #6, cheap and purely textual — the executor measures):** a lint over the commit range asserting that if `tests/fixtures/<stem>.gg` is modified and `tests/fixtures/runtime_snapshots/<stem>.out` exists, that `.out` is modified too. It needs an escape hatch for edits that provably cannot change output (comments, formatting) — and the escape hatch is the point: it forces the author to ASSERT that the output is unchanged rather than never being asked. It must FAIL against `4a5c6dad1` (Core #6: verify the guard on the commit that motivated it).

  ⭐ **THE NAIVE LINT WAS PROTOTYPED BY HAND OVER R50's OWN RANGE, AND ITS FALSE-POSITIVE RATE IS 2 OF 3.**
  Pairing *"`<stem>.gg` modified"* with *"`<stem>.out` not modified"* across `bee51d536..HEAD` flags **three**
  fixtures: `newtype` (the REAL one) plus `cow_for_bare_resource_elem_materialize` and

  ⇒ **TWO CONCLUSIONS, BOTH MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED.** (1) **The existing detector is RELIABLE** — it
