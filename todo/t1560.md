- **🆕📐 [MED — A PRE-EXISTING CALLER-SIDE INCONSISTENCY, AND THE ONE PLACE A MEASURED FIX'S PREDICATE COULD BE WRONG. Named 2026-09-06 by R51 Track H's scout.]**

  Two param-registration paths disagree about what `MutPtr` in `fn_sigs` **means**:

  | path | `MutableBorrow` param | ⇒ `fn_sigs` entry |
  |---|---|---|
  | generic — `src/ir/lowering/generics/mod.rs:1859` | **wrapped** in `MutPtr` | `MutPtr(T)` |
  | non-generic — `src/ir/lowering/mod.rs:679` | **not** wrapped | plain |

  ⇒ on the generic path, `MutPtr(T)` in `fn_sigs` is ambiguous between **`T &`** (a mutable borrow of an owned value) and **`MutRef[T]`** (a declared borrow parameter). Regenerate the two sites: `grep -rn 'MutableBorrow' src/ir/lowering/generics/mod.rs src/ir/lowering/mod.rs`.

  ⭐ **WHY IT IS FILED SEPARATELY FROM THE FIX THAT SURFACED IT.** Track H's call-site consult reads `fn_sigs` and sits in the **`Ownership::Borrow`** arm, which `&` cannot reach — so the ambiguity is believed not to bite it. ⚠ **BELIEVED, on a reading.** It is the single place that fix's predicate could be wrong, and it is a **pre-existing** defect either way: it is wrong independently of whether anything currently consults it.

  **INTENDED:** one spelling per axis (Layering rule 3 — *one source of truth per axis, read through one accessor*). Either both paths wrap, or neither does, and a declared `MutRef[T]` param is distinguishable from a `T &` borrow at every consumer.
