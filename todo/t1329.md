  ```gorget
  struct H:
      Box[String] b
  Box[String] take(H &h):
      return h.b
  ```

  `gorget_string_free` ← `Box__GorgetString__drop` ← `H__drop` ← `main`, freed by
  `__gorget_box_free_GorgetString`. **Without ASan:** `gorget_string_free invariant: cap=<garbage> but
  alloc=NULL`, rc 1.

  `Some(…)`, `d.put(…)` all give `E_MoveWithoutOperator` rc 1 at HEAD. ***RETURN IS THE ONE THAT GETS THROUGH,
  AND IT IS THE ONE THAT IS MEMORY-UNSAFE.***
