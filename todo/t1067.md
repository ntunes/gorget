  - **⛔ ALL THREE ANSWERS ARE BLOCKED, and this is why the item is filed rather than fixed.** An implicit CLONE breaches the ratified single-owner-by-design carve-out (`AGENTS.md` §Ownership at Consuming Positions: closures / `Callable[T]` / `Owned[T]` / `Box[T]` / `Task` / `TaskGroup` / `Guard` / `Mutex`/`RWLock` have *"no IMPLICIT-copy path in the lowering"*). An implicit MOVE breaches the same carve-out and silently consumes under D31 full-strict. A REJECTION cannot be acted on: `(^c5)(int y): …` is `expected ')'`, `^(int y): …` is `E_ClosureKindMismatch`, and **D7's per-variable capture list — the construct that would let a user say "capture `c5` by value" — is UNIMPLEMENTED** (`docs/define-gorget/decisions.md`).
