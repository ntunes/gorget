- **🆕🚨 [HIGH — SIGSEGV FROM A FIVE-LINE PROGRAM WITH NO FUNCTION CALL IN IT, both backends. Found 2026-09-05 by R50 Track G while giving the `(Trivial, Trivial)` refcount-handle row of `src/ir/types.rs`'s ownership table a disposition in topic 30's scope disclosure.]**

  ```gorget
  from std.sync import Shared

  void main():
      Shared[int] a = Shared[int](7)
      Shared[int] b = a          # <-- the bind is the trigger
      print(f"{a.get()}")
      print(f"{b.get()}")
  ```

  **MEASURED AT `7f68508b0`:** builds clean on both backends, then **rc 139 on C and rc 139 on LLVM**. Under `--sanitize`: `AddressSanitizer: SEGV ... in Shared__int64_t__get`. **ISOLATED — delete the `Shared[int] b = a` line and the identical program prints `7` at rc 0.** So it is the BARE BIND, not the construction, not `.get()`, and not the double print.

  **DISCRIMINATOR — this is a sibling of [[t0108]], NOT a duplicate, and the difference decides whether a fix reaches it.** `t0108` is scoped, in its own words, to the *"refcount PARAM / boundary ownership model"*: its mechanism is `needs_param_drop` (`src/ir/types.rs`) being unable to tell bare `Shared s` (borrow) from `Shared !s` (owned), and **all three of its repros are calls** — `takes(s)` then `s.strong_count()`, the callee dropping at scope exit. **This shape has no function, no parameter and no boundary.** An executor scoped to `needs_param_drop` and `register_param` would fix `t0108`'s three repros and leave this one segfaulting. It is also **strictly smaller than every repro `t0108` carries**, which makes it the better cell to pin the family's fix against.
  ⊕ Not [[t0620]] either: that is `Weak[T].upgrade()`-in-match **LEAKING** (never drop-registered). This is the opposite direction — drop-registered **twice**.

  **SUSPECTED MECHANISM, NOT YET PROTOTYPED — state it as suspicion.** `Shared` is `(CopySemantics::Trivial, DropStrategy::Trivial(fn))`: copyable at GIR level, decrement on drop. A bare bind therefore takes the CoW default-borrow path and copies the handle by value, but the drop elaborator registers both names because `drop_strategy != None` (the contract in `src/ir/types.rs`'s "Drop contract" comment). Two decrements against one increment frees the control block early. Core #3: the incref belongs at the second name's BIRTH.

  **WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND THE ONE PROGRAM.** `docs/book/` teaches `Shared` as the ordinary way to share a value, and binding one to a second name is the most obvious thing a reader will type next. ⊕ It also closes off a corpus cell: topic 30 cannot probe the refcount-handle row of the ownership axis while the shape crashes, which is recorded in `scripts/gen_value_semantics_cells.py`'s SCOPE LIMITS block and in [[t1384]].

  **OWED WITH THE FIX:** a `known_gaps/` repro asserting the intended `7 / 7`, plus siblings for `Weak` and `Channel` — the three share the `(Trivial, Trivial)` row and the arm-count discipline applies (Core #4).
