- **🆕📐 [MED — A MEASUREMENT TRAP THAT MANUFACTURES FALSE NEGATIVES. Re-measured 2026-09-06 by R51 Track F's brief-review pass 2; owned by `[[t0028]]`, which is open and was uncited.]**

  Readiness (1) requires *"every measurement carries a FIRE COUNT proving the mechanism executed."* ⛔ **A fire count collected through `cargo test` proves nothing: the harness swallows `gg`'s stderr**, so an `eprintln!` probe on a live branch reports **0**.

  ⚠ **MEASURED CONSEQUENCE: a positive control printed 0 twice, and the branch was TWICE DECLARED DEAD** before the trap was recognised. A fire count is supposed to be the thing that *cannot* be faked by a plausible story — this one can.

  ⇒ **COLLECT FIRE COUNTS FROM A DIRECT `gg build`, never through `cargo test`.** And a probe that reports 0 owes a **positive control** in the same run: if the control also reports 0, the instrument is blind and the measurement is void — not evidence of absence.

  ⊕ `[[t0028]]` also owns the census instrument for this area (1980 calls / 241 `Ptr` branch / 123 early return over 300 fixtures) and the layering precedent, and should be cited by any work touching `auto_clone_if_ptr`'s carve-out.

  ---
  ## ⊕ THE SAME TRAP IN A SECOND COSTUME: **RIGHT INSTRUMENT, WRONG PHASE** — measured 2026-09-06

  A reviewer ran `--clones=stats`, saw **no output**, and concluded `map_clone=0`. **False.** `[clone-stats]` is a **runtime `atexit` report**, and the output being read was the **build**'s. The instrument was correct and was pointed at the wrong phase of the pipeline.

  ⭐ **THE POSITIVE CONTROL IS WHAT CAUGHT IT — it also printed nothing.** ⇒ **the rule generalises past stderr-swallowing: a probe reporting 0 owes a positive control in the same run, whatever the reason for the 0 might be.** A silent instrument and an absent phenomenon are indistinguishable without one, and the failure is not always the harness eating output — sometimes you are simply reading the wrong process.
