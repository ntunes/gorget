- **🆕📐 [MED — FEATURE GAP, owner-ratified 2026-08-26: EXTEND THE FALLIBLE-ARITH OPERATORS `/!` and `%!` TO FLOATS] A float divide has NO CHECKED FORM today.** The D26 fallible operators (`+! -! *! /! %! <<! >>!`) surface faults into the `throws ArithError` channel instead of trapping, but they are **INTEGER-ONLY in v1** (`docs/language-reference.md:1655`, "Integer operands"). ⚖ **RATIFIED CONTEXT:** the owner ruled the same day that plain float `/ 0.0` correctly follows IEEE 754 (`±inf`, `NaN`) and does NOT trap — integer division by zero has no answer in the integer domain, float division by zero has a defined one. **That makes the reference-grade shape `/` follows IEEE, `/!` gives the checked answer on demand** — Python's safety as an OPT-IN, without taxing every divide with a branch (the "very fast" pillar) or making the tensor/dataframe libraries unusable. **INTENDED:** `a /! b` on floats surfaces `ArithError.DivByZero` for a zero divisor; whether NaN *production* (`0.0/0.0`, `inf - inf`) should also surface an error is an OPEN SUB-QUESTION — NaN is a value, not a fault, so a separate `ArithError` variant or a `NaN`-producing predicate may be the better shape. **Owed:** design the float semantics, extend the operators, cross-lane fixtures, and a doc-write-through to `language-reference.md:1655` + `language-design.md:231` (both of which currently say integer-only).
