- **🆕🚨 [HIGH — THE DEBT-BURN-DOWN CHANNEL ALREADY EXISTS, IS MEASURED EVERY ROUND, AND IS DISCARDED. Found 2026-09-09 by a scout sent to design something else.]**

  `adjudicate_leaks` in `scripts/sanitize_sweep.sh` is a pure function of (allowlist, verdicts, outdir) and writes seven classification files, among them **`fixed_leak`** — allowlist rows the sweep measured as **no longer leaking** — and **`shrunk_class`**, which carries the exact reduced count per class (`<class> x1 (row says x2)`).

  ⛔ **NOTHING READS EITHER.** `OUT` defaults to `/tmp/sanitize_sweep_$$`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml` invokes the sweep **bare**, and the round-close battery does too. Regenerate: `grep -n 'OUT=\|retire_due\|fixed_leak\|shrunk_class' scripts/sanitize_sweep.sh` and `grep -n 'sanitize_sweep' .github/workflows/ci.yml`. The only output anyone acts on is the fatal subset re-printed for **cited** rows — **14 of 423 pairs (3.3%)**. The other 96.7% of the adjudication is computed and deleted.

  ⭐ **WHY THIS IS HIGH.** The owner's standing directive is to **burn all known failures to 0**, and the leak allowlist is the largest single debt axis in the tree. The instrument that says *which rows can go* already runs every round. We are paying for the measurement and discarding the answer, then shrinking the list by hand when someone notices — which is why the four pins tracking it account for ~74% of all guard-pin churn (`[[t1628]]`).

  **A LIVE INSTANCE, measured:** `dict_iter_enumerate` (`tests/sanitize/LEAK_ALLOWLIST.txt`, uncited) came back `CLEAN/MEASURED` — `fixed_leak` names it, and it is still in the allowlist. ⚠ **Caveat the sweep states about itself:** a frame rename reads identically to a fix, so a row must not be retired on this signal alone — `new_class` empty is the corroborating precondition (a rename fires `new_class` **and** `shrunk_class` together).

  **INTENDED:**
  1. **Persist the verdict** — give the round-close and CI sweep invocations an explicit `OUT`. One line; the work is already done.
  2. **Surface `fixed_leak` + `shrunk_class` at round close** as a burn-down proposal (a table, not a gate — a red here would punish honest measurement).
  3. Feed it to `[[t1628]]`'s bless, which needs exactly these two fields as its decision input.

  ⚠ **DO NOT turn this into a gate that reds when a row looks retirable.** The rename hazard above makes a naive fatal wrong, and the round-lifecycle rule is explicit that discovery must never be punished. It is a **measurement that produces a worklist**.
